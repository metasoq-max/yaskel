Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled on yassinekelai.com, the website of Yassine Kelai, an independent digital publishing business based in Morocco and operating online.

1. Information Collection

This website collects information that visitors voluntarily provide through direct contact, such as name, email address, subject, and message content submitted through the contact form or email communication.

2. Contact Form Submissions

Information submitted through the contact form is used only to review and respond to business-related inquiries. Visitors should avoid sending sensitive personal or financial information through the form.

3. Communication

Contact details provided by visitors may be used to respond to questions, follow up on inquiries, and maintain business communication related to publishing activities.

4. Data Protection

Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to protect information from unauthorized access, misuse, or loss. While care is taken to protect data, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed as fully secure.

5. Cookies and Basic Analytics

This website may use essential cookies or basic analytics tools to support functionality and understand website usage trends. These tools are used in a general manner and are not intended to profile individuals.

6. Third-Party Platforms

Publishing and distribution activities may involve third-party online marketplaces and related services. Those platforms operate under their own privacy policies and terms. Visitors should review third-party policies directly when using those services.

7. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when needed to reflect operational or legal changes. Updates are posted on this page with a revised effective date.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact: contact@yassinekelai.com.

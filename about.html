About Yassine Kelai

Independent digital publisher based in Morocco and operating online.

Professional Profile

Yassine Kelai is an independent digital publisher focused on practical, reader-oriented self-publishing projects. The business is operated as a solo publishing activity, with each title researched, prepared, and managed directly by the owner.

Business Activity

The publishing activity covers project planning, interior structuring, formatting, cover preparation, listing setup, and post-publication catalog maintenance. Main categories include journals, planners, activity books, coloring books, notebooks, and low-content books.

Operating Model

The business operates online from Morocco and distributes publications through established digital marketplaces and related publishing platforms.

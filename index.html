Business Introduction

Yassine Kelai is an independent digital publisher operating a solo self-publishing business. The activity focuses on planning, producing, and managing book projects for online publication, with attention to clear formatting, useful structure, and consistent quality.

What I Do

Publishing Research

I review category demand, reader behavior, and practical topics to guide each publishing project.

Content Development

I design interiors and production-ready files for structured, easy-to-use print and digital book formats.

Portfolio Management

I maintain publication listings, optimize metadata, and monitor catalog performance over time.

Publishing Focus

My catalog includes practical formats that support planning, learning, creativity, and daily use.

  • Journals
  • Planners
  • Activity Books
  • Coloring Books
  • Notebooks
  • Low-Content Books

Business Model

This business operates through online self-publishing channels, where each title is prepared, published, and maintained as part of a long-term digital catalog strategy.

This business generates revenue through digital publishing and royalties from platforms such as Amazon KDP.

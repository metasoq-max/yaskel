Business Profile

About Yassine Kelai

Independent digital publishing focused on practical books for online distribution.

Founder Profile

My name is Yassine Kelai, and I run a solo digital publishing business under my own name. I focus on building and maintaining structured publication projects that are useful, readable, and professionally formatted for marketplace distribution.

Business Activity

The core business activity is self-publishing. This includes selecting viable publication concepts, developing layouts, preparing final files, and publishing titles through established online book platforms. The catalog includes journals, planners, activity books, coloring books, notebooks, and other low-content formats.

How Projects Are Managed

Each project follows a clear process: topic planning, content structure design, interior and cover preparation, quality review, publication setup, and post-launch management. After release, listings are monitored and updated when needed to maintain consistency and reader clarity.

Operating Model

This business is based in Morocco and operates online. Publishing operations, communication, and catalog management are handled digitally, with distribution taking place through third-party online marketplaces.
